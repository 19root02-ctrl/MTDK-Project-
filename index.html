Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to participate in IMTSE?

All school students from Class I to Class X enrolled in recognized schools across any board (State, CBSE, ICSE) can participate.

What is the syllabus of the examination?

The syllabus covers age-appropriate Science, Mathematics, and Mental Ability/Logical Reasoning matching their current standards.

Can I change the medium of exam after registering?

No, the medium (English, Semi-English, Marathi) selected during registration will be printed on the admit card and cannot be changed.

Is the registration fee refundable?

As per regulation 1, the registration/examination fee is non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances.

Exam Rules & Regulations

Please read the rules carefully. You must accept them at the bottom to open the Online Application Form.

  1. The examination fee is non-refundable and non-transferable.
  2. Students must carry their Admit Card and School ID/Aadhaar Card on the examination day.
  3. Candidates should report to the examination centre 30 minutes before the reporting time.
  4. Entry to the examination hall after the scheduled time may not be permitted.
  5. Students must bring blue/black ball pen, pencil, eraser, sharpener, and other required stationery.
  6. Mobile phones, smart watches, calculators, books, notes, or any electronic devices are strictly prohibited.
  7. Candidates must follow the instructions given by the invigilators throughout the examination.
  8. Any form of malpractice, cheating, or misconduct will lead to immediate disqualification.
  9. The examination centre allotted by the organisers shall be final. Changes, if any, will be communicated in advance.
  10. In unavoidable circumstances, the organisers reserve the right to change the examination date, time, or venue.
  11. The decision of the examination committee shall be final and binding in all matters.
  12. Results will be declared on the official website/official communication channel on the notified date.
  13. Merit certificates, medals, scholarships, and prizes will be awarded according to the official merit list.
  14. Parents/Guardians should ensure that the information provided in the application form is correct.
  15. The organisers are not responsible for the loss of any personal belongings at the examination centre.
  16. Candidates must maintain discipline and follow all examination rules.
  17. Any dispute regarding the examination shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the organiser's district court.
  18. Submission of the application form implies acceptance of all the above rules and regulations.
